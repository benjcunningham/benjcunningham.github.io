Despite the name, Kodak rate its true speed at EI 800 in developers like DD-X,
so the box speed of 3200 is already a two-stop push. Most people shoot it at
1600 or 3200.

Eastman Kodak have sold this as Ektapan P3200 since 2026. It's widely
assumed to be the same film, but neither Kodak company has confirmed it.
