Neither Foma nor Ilford publish a DD-X time, so both times come from the
Massive Dev Chart. Its EI 400 entries range from 9:00 to 11:30; 9:00 is the
most common. Foma say the film tolerates two stops of underexposure without
any change in development, so if EI 400 negatives come out dense, try the box
speed time.

Box speed is much shorter than the Ilford and Kodak films, so don't mix them
up.
