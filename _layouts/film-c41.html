Process

  1. Heat the TCS-1000 bath to 102°F with the developer and blix bottles in it.
  2. Develop for the time above. Agitate continuously for the first 10 seconds, then 4 inversion cycles every 30 seconds. Pour back.
  3. Blix for 8 minutes, agitating the same way. Pour back.
  4. Wash for 3 minutes in running water, or fill and empty the tank 7 times, at 75–105°F. The lid can come off from here.
  5. Dry hanging.
