Process

  1. Develop for the time above. Invert 4 times in the first 10 seconds of each minute, then tap. Start draining 10 seconds early. Toss the developer.
  2. Stop for {{ data.stop.time }}, agitating. Pour back.
  3. Fix for {{ data.fixer.time }}, agitating like the developer. Pour back.
  4. Wash in three fills of fresh water, inverting 5, 10, then 20 times.
  5. Rinse in Ilfotol for a minute, no agitation.
  6. Dry hanging, after a wipe with a squeegee.
